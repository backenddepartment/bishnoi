# Bishnoi research base

Source material for the `/bishnoi/*` pages. Nothing historical ships to the site
unless it appears here with a grade.

This matters more than it normally would. The site publishes no author byline and
has no community reviewer, so sourcing rigour is the only thing standing behind
every claim we make about a living community's history. Grade conservatively. When
in doubt, drop a tier.

## Grading scale

| Grade | Meaning | How it may appear on the site |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Established** | Multiple independent sources agree; no meaningful dispute. | State plainly. |
| **B — Tradition** | Widely held within the community and widely repeated, but not independently documented. | State, attributed: "community tradition records…", "as the community remembers it…". |
| **C — Disputed** | Sources materially disagree, or one source hedges. | Present both readings. Never pick a side silently. |
| **D — Do not publish** | Popular claim with no adequate support, or demonstrably wrong. | Omit. If it is currently live on the site, remove it. |

A **B** stated as an **A** is the specific failure mode this register exists to
prevent: turning a community's memory of itself into asserted history, under a
corporate domain, with nobody's name on it.

## Known ceiling on sources

The strongest material for this subject is not reachable from open web search:

- the 1961 Census of India monograph on the Bishnoi
- the 1965 Mukam village socio-economic survey
- paywalled scholarship, including the *Journal of Vaishnava Studies* article on
  Bishnoi eco-theology

Everything graded here rests on openly verifiable sources. Chapters flag where a
claim would move up a grade if one of the above were consulted. Those are the
places to spend a library visit.

## Standing editorial rules

1. **The tradition is old; the businesses are not.** Never let a sentence be read
   as the company claiming a 500-year history.
2. **We do not own this legacy.** The Bishnoi community is far larger than this
   company and mostly unconnected to it. Say so where it is relevant.
3. **No retroactive modern labels.** "India's first environmentalists" applies a
   20th-century concept to a 15th-century religious community. Describe what the
   tradition actually holds and let the reader draw the comparison.
4. **Attribute contested things in the sentence, not in a footnote.**
